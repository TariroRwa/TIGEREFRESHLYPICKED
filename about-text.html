Our Story

Cultivating fresh experiences daily

Welcome to Tigere Freshly Picked. As a proudly female-owned and operated Zimbabwean Organic Produce Company. We are passionate about bringing fresh, healthy, and organic produce straight from our local fields directly to your kitchen table. We blend dedicated local stewardship with a commitment to feeding our community.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to top-tier, nutrient-rich food. We ensure that our vegetables, fruits, and farm hampers remain affordable while maintaining the absolute highest standards of quality in the Zimbabwean market.

What We Offer

From custom vegetable hampers tailored specifically to your family's needs, to bulk farm orders for local businesses, we hand-pick every item with care to guarantee ultimate freshness.
