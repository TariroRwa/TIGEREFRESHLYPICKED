About Us

From A Local Zimbabwean Farm Straight to Your Kitchen. Enjoy farm-to-table freshness without the hassle. We hand-pick our 100% organic fruits and vegetables which are locally grown. So your family eats healthier, crispier, and more nutritious meals every single day. We ofer bulk purchases, as well as minimum order purchases. Tigere Freshly Picked also offers customized vegetable hampers as per order. Whatever your vegetable needs are, we are at your service.
